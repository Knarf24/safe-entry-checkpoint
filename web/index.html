🚪 Safe Entry Checkpoint

A door camera for clinics, shops and offices. It finds every face, checks each one for a mask, keeps count of how many people are inside, and says the result out loud.

🐍 Written in Python (OpenCV + NumPy), running right here in your browser with Pyodide. Photos never leave your device.

How it works

  1. Face detection and counting: OpenCV's YuNet face detector finds every face in the photo.
  2. Mask check: each face goes through a MobileNetV2 network, and a small classifier trained on about 4,000 photos of people with and without masks decides mask or no mask (about 99% accurate in testing). A mask pulled down under the chin counts as no mask.
  3. Decision: everyone must wear a mask (if required) and there must be room inside. People who pass are added to the count.
  4. Speech: Python tells your browser's built-in voice what to say.

All of this is Python (checkpoint.py and door.py) running in your browser through Pyodide. See the code on GitHub.
